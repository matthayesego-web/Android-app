# TornFCA v0.9.30 — 2026-08-17

- Simplified the authenticated mobile shell around five member tabs: Home, Faction, War, Training and More; verified faction leaders receive Leadership as a sixth tab.
- Reduced Home to a short personal starting point instead of duplicating the full feature catalog.
- Restored the Torn profile avatar to the persistent identity header by reusing the existing `tornfca-profile-avatar` loading path.
- Made Training a first-class tab with Training Center, My Training Progress, and Guide & Training Management for verified leadership.
- Grouped Faction extras under a second-level Tools & Community menu.
- Grouped leadership features into People & Activity, War & Finance, and Faction Operations submenus.
- Kept the bottom navigation visible on the War landing page and redirected legacy War Center shortcuts back into the persistent War tab.
- Kept Ranked War and Territories as separate detail destinations underneath War.
- Added a dedicated Mobile Navigation Audit to prevent regressions in tab count, avatar presence, training discoverability, submenu grouping and War routing.
- Bumped Android versionCode to 47 and versionName to 0.9.30.

This remains a test candidate on `v0.9-faction-automation`; `main` is not promoted by this change.
