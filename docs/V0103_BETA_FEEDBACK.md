# TornFCA v0.10.3 Beta feedback pass

- Developer Lab removed from normal App Control; owner Developer Gate is hidden behind three taps on the command version badge.
- Operations featured card is repurposed from duplicate Ranked War content to Faction Announcements and its height is allowed to wrap content.
- Leadership Operations gains discoverable Faction Announcements and War Prep Management actions.
- War Prep completion remains local-first and resets by ranked-war ID.
- Community Backend v1.7.0 adds faction-scoped War Prep configuration and per-war status for TornFCA users.
- Leadership War Prep explicitly reports only members who opened/synced War Prep in TornFCA, not the full faction roster.
- Faction checklist configuration is isolated by freshly verified faction ID.
- App remains on the 0.10.x Beta line pending Google Play testing; this is not v1.0.
